Note: This dataset covers battery installations from July 2025 to August 2026 — 14 months of CER postcode-level data. 2025 contains 6 months (Jul–Dec, 73,970 installations) and 2026 contains 8 months (Jan–Aug, 111,086 installations), so raw year totals are not directly comparable. The 2026 projected full year of 166,629 is extrapolated from 8 months of data. The federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate, which started 1 July 2025, coincides with the beginning of this dataset and is a likely driver of the volumes recorded. Falling battery costs and changes to solar feed-in tariffs are also contributing factors. Data source: Clean Energy Regulator — cer.gov.au. Recent months are provisional — installers can lodge certificates some time after installation, so the latest figures typically rise with subsequent data releases and the 2026 projection is likely conservative.
